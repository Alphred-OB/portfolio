Erudite Academy.

A private online learning platform for Erudite Africa Network, built from scratch so every student's data stays with the people who teach them.

Client
Erudite Africa Network
My role
Designed and built it, end to end
Type
Online learning platform
Live
eruditeacademy.net
01

The brief

Erudite Africa Network trains people in tech, and they needed a proper home for it: one place to publish courses, enrol students, follow their progress and award certificates.

The quick option would have been to rent an existing course platform. EAN said no. Those platforms collect a lot of information about every learner, and once it is on someone else's servers you have very little say in what happens to it. For an organisation that asks people to trust it with their learning, that was not acceptable.

02

Why build our own

So we built Erudite Academy ourselves, and I was the one building it. Owning the platform meant EAN could decide everything:

  • Student data stays with EAN. No outside company collecting or keeping learner information.
  • Only what is needed is collected. Nothing gathered just because a platform wants it.
  • The brand is theirs. Their look, their courses, their programmes, like The Koder Xperience.
  • Security from day one. Protected accounts and passwords, safe forms and regular backups.
03

The hardest part: certificates

A certificate is the thing students share with employers, family and on LinkedIn. It has to look official every single time.

The problem

Every certificate carries different details: short names, very long names, different course titles and dates. A design that looks perfect for one student can break for the next, with text overflowing or squeezed out of place. And nobody should have to fix each one by hand.

What I did

I designed the certificate as a proper template, then built the system that fills it in: the student's name, the course and the date, generated automatically once the course is complete. The layout adjusts to fit the details, so it comes out clean and ready to download, whoever the student is.

04

The other hard part: people

Many of the students and staff had never used a learning platform before. If they could not find their way around, the rest would not matter.

The problem

Technical menus, unclear buttons and too many options on one screen left people lost. For a first time user, getting stuck once is often enough to give up.

What I did

I kept every screen simple. Plain words instead of technical labels, one clear next step at a time, and progress always visible, so people know where they are and what to do next. It works just as well on a phone, where most students learn.

05

The result

EAN now runs its training on a platform it fully owns.

  • Courses, students and certificates all in one place.
  • Certificates created automatically, with no manual design work.
  • Learner data that stays private and under EAN's control.
  • A platform that is easy for first time users on any device.
