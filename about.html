Security built in

Security is notan add-on.

Cyber security, UMaT

With a background in cyber security, every system I build ships with these protections from day one. You never have to ask for them.

  • Encrypted connections

    HTTPS everywhere, so data moving between your users and your system cannot be read or changed.

  • Protected passwords

    Passwords are hashed and never stored as plain text, so a leak does not expose your users.

  • Attack-proof forms

    Every form and input is checked to block SQL injection, cross site scripting and forged requests.

  • Login defence

    Rate limits and lockouts stop brute force attacks, with two-step login where it matters.

  • Right access, right people

    Role based permissions mean staff only see and change what their job needs.

  • Backups and monitoring

    Regular backups and uptime checks, so problems are caught early and data can be restored.
