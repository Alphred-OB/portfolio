A few years ago, if someone in Accra needed a caterer, they typed "caterers near me" into Google and scrolled through ten blue links. Today a growing number of people skip that and just ask. They open ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity or the AI answer at the top of Google and type "Who is a reliable caterer in Accra for a wedding of 200 people?"

The AI does not give them ten links. It gives them an answer, usually with two or three names. If your business is not one of those names, for that person you do not exist.

That is what AEO is about.

## SEO and AEO in plain words

**SEO (search engine optimisation)** is making your website easy for Google to find, understand and rank, so you show up when people search.

**AEO (answer engine optimisation)** is making your business easy for AI assistants to understand and trust, so they mention you when people ask a question.

They are not rivals. AEO is built on top of good SEO. AI tools read the same web that Google reads, and many of them lean on search results to find their answers. If Google cannot make sense of your site, an AI will not either.

## Why it matters, especially for small businesses

- **Fewer clicks, bigger stakes.** When the answer is right there on the screen, people click less. Being the name inside the answer matters more than being the fifth link.
- **Questions are getting more specific.** People now ask full questions with location, budget and needs in them. Businesses that clearly explain who they serve, where and for how much are easier to recommend.
- **It levels the field.** An AI does not care how big your advertising budget is. It cares whether it can find clear, consistent, trustworthy information about you.

## What actually helps

This is the checklist I work through for clients. None of it is magic, and most of it is simply being clear.

1. **Say exactly what you do.** Your homepage should say what you offer, who it is for and where you operate, in the first few lines. "We help people grow" gives an AI nothing to work with. "Wedding and event catering in Accra and Tema for 50 to 500 guests" does.
2. **Answer real questions on your site.** Look at what customers ask you on WhatsApp and the phone, and answer those questions on your website in plain language. A good FAQ is one of the most useful pages you can have.
3. **Add structured data.** This is a small block of hidden code that tells search engines and AI tools, in their own language, what your business is, where it is, your opening hours and your contact details. Visitors never see it. Machines read it first.
4. **Keep your details the same everywhere.** Your business name, phone number and address should match on your website, Google Business Profile, Facebook, Instagram and directories. Mismatched details make you look less trustworthy to machines, just like they would to a person.
5. **Collect genuine reviews.** AI tools pay attention to what customers say about you on Google, Trustpilot and elsewhere. Ask happy customers to leave a review. Reply to them, including the bad ones.
6. **Add a sitemap and robots.txt.** A sitemap is a list of all your pages so nothing gets missed. robots.txt tells visiting bots what they are allowed to read. A surprising number of sites accidentally block the very tools they want to be found by.
7. **Add an llms.txt file.** This is a newer idea: a short plain text summary of your business and your most important pages, written for AI tools to read. It is not a standard every AI follows yet, but it takes ten minutes to write and it cannot hurt.
8. **Make the site fast and mobile friendly.** Slow, broken pages get ranked lower by search engines, which means AI tools are less likely to come across them.

## What does not work

Stuffing keywords, publishing hundreds of thin AI written pages, or making claims you cannot back up. AI tools are getting better at ignoring noise, and the cost of being caught out is your reputation.

## Where to start today

If you only do three things: make your homepage say plainly what you do and where, fill out your Google Business Profile completely, and ask five happy customers for a review this week.
